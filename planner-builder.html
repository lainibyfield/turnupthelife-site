Build a Day.

No recipe — and no plate size — belongs to a time of day. Pick three anchors by weight class plus a module; eat them whenever your day wants them. The Planner watches protein, calories, and rotation. Aim for at least three protein families.

A common rhythm puts the heavy anchor at midday and something lighter at night — but that is a suggestion, not a rule. Want a light day? Build three lighter anchors. It is your day.
