Lesson 03 · Travel Workout 1

Create
Tension.

A band only feels heavy if you make it heavy. That's the whole lesson. Tension comes from where you stand, how much slack you take out, how slowly you return, and how long you squeeze — not from the band alone.

Lesson 2 taught you to move weight with power. Lesson 3 teaches you to create resistance out of thin air — so a backpack full of rubber can replace a gym.

PrincipleMake bands feel heavy.
RoleFull body, biased toward pull and upper back.
Time40–50 minutes.
EquipmentLight, medium, and heavy long bands or tubes. A door anchor if you have one.
MoodNew · travel · self-directed.

Band rules

  • Light band — shoulders, pull-aparts, lateral raises.
  • Medium band — chest press, overhead press, face pulls.
  • Heavy band — squats, rows, good mornings, glute bridges.
  • A set only counts if the last few reps are hard and clean.
  • If you hit 25 reps easily, the band is too light or too slack. Step wider. Shorten the band. Take out the slack.

Equipment note
This workout assumes long bands or tubes. If all you packed are mini-loops, the squats, rows, presses, and good mornings need a swap — the anchor lines below won't work as written.

How travel week works

Two workouts, each done twice. Not four new workouts.

Day 1Create Tension
Day 2Rest — walk, swim, shop, enjoy the trip
Day 3Pack Light, Train Hard
Day 4Rest — fun movement
Day 5Create Tension — progressed
Day 6Rest — fun movement
Day 7Pack Light, Train Hard — progressed

"Progressed" never means new exercises. Pick one per exercise: next heavier band · shorten the band · add a 2-second squeeze · add a 3-second return · keep tension between reps · add 2–5 reps · shorten transitions only if form stays clean.

Both workouts once ≈ 10 hard sets per major muscle group. Both twice ≈ 20. That's enough. Don't turn your trip into punishment.

GET LOW

Move Prep

5 min

Grounded and short. Warm up; don't tire out.

  • Deep squat hold — 30 sec
  • 90/90 hip switches — 8 each side. Turn the hips. Keep the chest proud. watch
  • Hip hinge reach — 10 reps. Hips back, spine long. watch
  • Shoulder circles — 10 each direction.
BUILD

Core

5–6 min

Protect the back. Core is spinal organization, not ab punishment.

Band-Resisted Dead Bugdeep core — ribs and pelvis control

Move the limbs. Don't move the spine.

Sets 2Reps 8–10 / sideBand light or medium

Anchor / setupBand anchored low behind your head (door, bed leg, or heavy furniture), held with tension above your chest. No anchor? Hold both ends overhead and press them apart for tension.

  • Hold the band with tension above your chest.
  • Reach one leg at a time.
  • Exhale as the leg reaches.
  • Stop if the low back starts taking over.

Feel: deep belly, ribs pinned.   Not: low back arching off the floor.

Anchor note
Test the anchor with a few gentle pulls before you lie down under it.

Watch the form

Side Planklateral core — hip and rib stack

Lift the bottom waist.

Sets 2Hold 15–25 sec / side
  • Stack ribs over hips.
  • Knees down is fine.
  • No sagging into the shoulder.

Shoulder note
If the bottom shoulder complains, drop to knees or shorten the hold.

BUILD

Legs

16–18 min

A1Band Split Squatquad and glute work — one foot forward, one foot back

Drop straight down. Own the front leg.

Sets 3 / sideReps 10–15Band medium or heavyEffort 1–2 good reps left

Anchor / setupBand under the front foot only. Hold both ends at your shoulders.

  • Stand on the band with the front foot.
  • Hold the band at your shoulders.
  • Bend both knees and lower with control.
  • Front knee tracks with the toes.
  • Use a wall if balance gets annoying.

Feel: front thigh and glute.   Not: sharp knee pain.

Knee note
Shorten the range if the knee feels sharp or sketchy.

If too easy: step wider on the band to take out slack.

Watch the form

A2Band Squatquad and glute volume

Push the floor away.

Sets 2Reps 15–20Band heavyPace 3 down · 1 hold · stand strong

Anchor / setupBand under both feet. Hold the ends at your shoulders or chest.

  • Stand on the band, feet about shoulder-width.
  • Hold it at shoulders or chest.
  • Keep ribs stacked.
  • Don't let the band fold you forward.

Feel: thighs and glutes.   Not: low back doing the standing.

Watch the form

A3Band Good Morninghamstrings and glutes 🌱 fertilizer

Close the car door with your butt.

Sets 3Reps 12–18Band heavyPace 3 down · 2 stretch · controlled up

Anchor / setupBand under both feet. Hold the ends at your shoulders — not across your neck.

  • Stand on the band.
  • Hold it at your shoulders, not your neck.
  • Hinge back with soft knees.
  • Stop before your back rounds.

Feel: hamstring stretch.   Not: low-back strain.

Back note
Hamstring stretch, yes. Low-back strain, no. If the back rounds, you went too far.

Watch the form

A4Band Glute Bridge or Frog Pumpglute squeeze 🌱 fertilizer

Small move. Big squeeze.

Sets 2Reps 20–30Band medium or heavy

Anchor / setupLong band across the hips, ends anchored under both feet. Or a loop above the knees, if that's the band you packed.

  • Band across hips if possible, anchored under the feet.
  • Or use a loop above the knees.
  • Squeeze at the top.
  • Don't arch your back to lift higher.

Feel: glutes burning.   Not: low back cramping.

BUILD

Push

6–8 min

Band Chest Presspush work without dumbbells

Ribs down. Press straight out.

Sets 4Reps 10–20Band mediumPace 3 return · press strong

Anchor / setupBand wraps around your upper back, one end in each hand. No anchor needed — your own back is the anchor.

  • Band behind your upper back, one end in each hand.
  • Press forward.
  • Return slowly; don't let the band snap you back.
  • Keep the ribs stacked — don't arch to press harder.

Feel: chest and front shoulder.   Not: pinching at the top of the shoulder.

Shoulder note
Keep elbows slightly below shoulder height if the shoulders complain.

Watch the form
BUILD

Pull

10–12 min

Today's bias. This is where bands genuinely beat dumbbells — the tension keeps climbing exactly where your back is strongest.

C1Band Rowmain pull work

Don't let the band win on the way back.

Sets 4Reps 12–20Band medium or heavyPace 2 pull · 2 squeeze · 3 return

Anchor / setupSit on the floor with the band around both feet, one end in each hand. Or stand hinged forward with the band under both feet.

  • Sit with the band around your feet, or stand hinged with it under your feet.
  • Pull the elbows back.
  • Squeeze the shoulder blades — like squeezing the juice out of a rolled mango.
  • Return slowly and keep tension the whole way.

Feel: mid-back between the shoulder blades.   Not: biceps doing all the work.

Watch the form

C2Band Face Pullupper-back and rear-shoulder reset

Pull the band to your face. Don't stick your face into the band.

Sets 2Reps 15–25Band light or mediumPace 2 pull · 2 squeeze · 3 returnEffort 2–3 good reps left

Anchor / setupDoor anchor or sturdy post at face height, or slightly above. Step back until there is light tension before rep one.

  • Anchor the band at face height or slightly above.
  • Step back until there is light tension.
  • Pull toward your face with elbows wide.
  • Think hands toward ears, shoulder blades back and down.
  • Return slowly. Don't let the band snap you forward.

Feel: rear shoulders and upper back.   Not: neck and traps shrugging.

Anchor note
Test the anchor with a few gentle pulls first. If it feels sketchy, swap for band pull-aparts — no anchor, hold the band in both hands at chest height.

Watch the form

Shoulders

6–8 min

D1Band Overhead Pressshoulders

Ribs down before the press.

Sets 3Reps 8–15Band light or medium

Anchor / setupBand under one foot (easier) or both feet (harder). Hands start at your shoulders.

  • Stand on the band.
  • Start with hands at the shoulders.
  • Press overhead without leaning back.
  • Use the lighter band if your neck joins in.

Feel: shoulders.   Not: low back arching, neck straining.

Watch the form

D2Band Lateral Raiseside delts

Lead with elbows. Heavy shoulders.

Sets 2Reps 12–20Band light

Anchor / setupBand under one foot (single-arm) or both feet (both arms). Hold the ends at your sides.

  • Stand on the band.
  • Raise the arms out to the side.
  • Stop before the traps take over.
  • Slow lower — that's where the work is.

Feel: side of the shoulder.   Not: shrugging into the neck.

RISE

Relaxed Finish

4–5 min

New lesson, new tool. Settle the body — don't chase one more thing.

  • Forward fold / hinge hang — 30 sec. Don't chase the floor. Chase the position. watch
  • 90/90 stretch — 30 sec / side. watch
  • Hero stretch or quad stretch — 30 sec / side. Breathe where you're tight. watch
  • Easy breathing — 3 slow breaths.

🥤 That's Lesson 3. Go get your drink — the band did what the dumbbells do.

When you repeat this on Day 5

Do not add exercises. Pick one progression per exercise:

  • Use the next heavier band.
  • Shorten the band (step wider, or choke up on the ends).
  • Add a 2-second squeeze at the hard end.
  • Add a 3-second return.
  • Keep tension between reps — never go slack.
  • Add 2–5 reps.
  • Reduce transition time, only if form stays clean.

Adjust range and depth based on how your knee, hip, shoulder, and back feel. Bands store energy — never let one snap back at you, and check any door anchor before you load it. Check with a professional first if anything hurts or you're unsure this suits you.
