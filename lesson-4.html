Lesson 04 · Turn Up the Life

Increase
Density.

More quality work in less time. Familiar exercises, purposeful pairings — you recover one muscle group while another one works.

Lesson 1 taught you to own the weight. Lesson 2 taught you to create power. Lesson 3 taught you to create tension. Lesson 4 changes none of those — it teaches you to recover while another muscle group works.

≈45–50 min · Two 10 lb dumbbells · sturdy chair or plyo box · optional kettlebell.

What the clock is for
The clock is here to remove empty space — not to make your form sloppy. Same clean reps, less standing around. If speed starts costing you control, you have missed the point of the lesson.

How a pair works
Do A1, then go straight into A2 with no rest between them. That is one round. Rest after the round, not inside it. Time all the rounds together and write the number down — that is what you try to beat next time, with the same clean reps.

Step 1 / 6GET LOW

Move Prep

6–7 min

Warm, not tired. The same warm-up as every lesson — learn it once, own it forever.

Before you begin

Today's warm-up prepares your body for today's workout — not for a flexibility contest.

  • Work only in a pain-free range.
  • Hold a chair, wall, or countertop anytime balance limits the movement.
  • Smaller movements done well beat bigger movements done poorly.
  • If something feels sharp, unstable, or wrong, reduce the range or skip that exercise and move on.
Timer

  • Wide deep-squat hold — 20–30 seconds. watch Mod: hold a chair or countertop, or don't squat as low.
  • Weight shifts in the wide squat — rock side to side, 8 each side. watch
  • Cossack shift (a.k.a. spider-man lunge) — stay low, let your hips travel side to side, 6–8 each way. Keep the working heel down; don't force depth. watchMod: stand a little taller, or hold a chair for balance.
  • Narrow deep-squat hold — 20 seconds. watch Mod: hold support, or place a rolled towel under your heels.
  • Staggered deep-squat hold — 15–20 seconds each side. watch Mod: shorten the stance and reduce the depth.
  • Knee-over-toe touches — 8–10 each side, 2-second pause. watchMod: let the knee travel only as far as comfortable, or hold a chair.
  • Assisted one-leg squat practice (hold something) — 3–5 clean each side. watchMod: use both hands for support, or squat to a higher chair.
  • Shoulder-blade push-ups — 10 reps. watchMod: perform against a wall, countertop, or bench.
  • Band pull-aparts or big shoulder circles — 15 reps. watchMod: use a lighter band, or reduce the range of motion.

Ready to build? If you're breathing a little harder and your joints feel smoother — not tired — you warmed up just right.

Pair A

10–12 min

Lower body + push. Your legs rest while your chest works, and the other way round — that is the whole trick.

Timer
3 rounds. A1 → straight into A2 = one round. Rest after the round, not inside it. Time all three.

A1Bulgarian Split Squatgoblet hold · back foot up on the chair or box

Imagine your zipper stays vertical.

Pace 3 down · 1 pause · controlled riseEffort 2 reps left in reserveReps 8–10 / legWeight 1 DB or KB at chest (goblet)
  • Back foot on the chair, box, or step; weight held at your chest in both hands (goblet).
  • Stay more upright than you think; let the front knee travel over the toes.
  • Drive through the middle of the foot.
  • The back leg helps with balance only.
Do thisBack foot on the chair or box, 1 DB or KB at your chest. 8–10 per leg.If that’s too difficultPut the weight down — bodyweight only, holding a chair for balance.If that’s too easySlow the lowering to 4 seconds, or add weight.
Watch the form

Pair B

10–12 min

Hinge + pull. The back of the legs works, then the back itself.

Timer
3 rounds. B1 → straight into B2 = one round. Time all three.

B1Deficit Single-Leg RDLstanding on the box for extra reach

Close the car door with your butt.

Pace 3 down · 2 stretch · controlled riseEffort 2 reps left in reserveReps 8–10 / legWeight 2 DBs🌱 fertilizer
  • Hinge from the hips — soft bend in the standing knee, long spine.
  • Hold something if balance starts limiting the exercise. Balance is not the point here; the hamstring is.
  • Stop before the back rounds.

Progression
Start on the floor with the working leg's toe touching down behind you for balance. Advance to standing on the box for a deeper stretch. Advance again to the full single-leg version — no toe down.

Do thisStanding on the box, 2 dumbbells, 8–10 per leg.If that’s too difficultOn the floor, with the working leg’s toe touching down behind you for balance.If that’s too easyFull single-leg version — the working leg lifts and extends, no toe down.
Watch the form

Pair C

8–10 min

Glutes + shoulders. Small movements, big squeezes — the easiest pair to rush, and the easiest to ruin by rushing.

Timer
2 rounds — 3 if quality holds. C1 → straight into C2 = one round. Add the third only if the reps still look like round one and the transitions stayed quick.

C1Frog Pumpsglutes — heels together, knees wide

Small movement. Big squeeze.

Pace 2 squeeze · controlled lowerReps 20–30Weight bodyweight🌱 fertilizer
  • On your back, soles of the feet together, knees falling open.
  • Drive through the outside edges of the feet; squeeze at the top for 2.
  • The range is small on purpose — the squeeze is the exercise, not the height.

Progression
Add one dumbbell across the hips only when you can complete all the prescribed reps with a full squeeze and control.

Do thisBodyweight — heels together, knees wide. 20–30 reps with a 2-second squeeze.If that’s too difficultMake the range smaller — lift an inch, keep the full squeeze.If that’s too easyAdd one dumbbell across the hips — only once you can finish all the reps with a full squeeze and control.
Watch the form
↓ straight into ↓

C2One-Arm Lateral Raiseshoulders — one side at a time

Lead with the elbow.

Pace 2 up · 1 hold · 3 downReps 10–15 / armWeight 1 DB
  • Heavy shoulders — do not let the neck take over.
  • Stop at shoulder height; the 3-second lower is where the work is.
Do thisOne dumbbell, 10–15 per arm. 2 up · 1 hold · 3 down.If that’s too difficultLighter weight, or stop at half height.If that’s too easySlow the lowering to 4 seconds.
Watch the form

Two rounds. Power, then control, then let the breathing settle before you stop.

Walk It Outuntil the breathing settles

Time 30–60 sec
  • Keep moving — recover without sitting down.

End standing

Stand tall. Take three slow breaths. Then ask yourself:

  • Did my reps stay clean?
  • Did my transitions improve?
  • Did I rush — or did I simply waste less time?
