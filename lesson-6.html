Lesson 06 · Travel Workout 2

Pack Light,
Train Hard.

This one proves the point: bands can replace the workout. Not a substitute, not a holding pattern — a complete session in a hotel room, out of a backpack, with nothing bolted to the floor.

Lesson 3 taught you to make a band feel heavy. Lesson 6 asks you to run a whole balanced day on that skill alone.

PrincipleBands can replace the workout.
RoleBalanced full body.
Time40–55 minutes.
EquipmentLight, medium, and heavy long bands or tubes. A safe anchor if you have one.
MoodTravel · familiar · self-directed.

Band rules

  • Light band — shoulders, pull-aparts, lateral raises.
  • Medium band — chest press, overhead press, face pulls.
  • Heavy band — squats, rows, good mornings, glute bridges.
  • A set only counts if the last few reps are hard and clean.
  • If you hit 25 reps easily, the band is too light or too slack. Step wider. Shorten the band. Take out the slack.

Equipment note
Assumes long bands or tubes. Mini-loops only? The squats, rows, presses, and good mornings need a swap — the anchor lines below won't work as written.

How travel week works

Two workouts, each done twice. Not four new workouts.

Day 1Create Tension
Day 2Rest — walk, swim, shop, enjoy the trip
Day 3Pack Light, Train Hard
Day 4Rest — fun movement
Day 5Create Tension — progressed
Day 6Rest — fun movement
Day 7Pack Light, Train Hard — progressed

"Progressed" never means new exercises. Pick one per exercise: next heavier band · shorten the band · add a 2-second squeeze · add a 3-second return · keep tension between reps · add 2–5 reps · shorten transitions only if form stays clean.

Both workouts once ≈ 10 hard sets per major muscle group. Both twice ≈ 20. That's enough. Don't turn your trip into punishment.

GET LOW

Move Prep

5 min

Grounded and short. Warm up; don’t tire out.

  • Deep squat hold — 30 sec
  • Knee-over-toe touches — 8 / side. watch
  • Hip hinge reach — 10 reps. Hips back, spine long. watch
  • Shoulder circles — 10 each way.
BUILD

Core

6–7 min

Protect the back. Brace, then the one curl, then the lateral hips.

Bear Hoverclosed-chain brace

Knees barely up. Back wide.

Sets 2Hold 15–25 sec
  • Hands under shoulders.
  • Knees hover one inch.
  • Ribs stay tucked.
  • Stop before your back sags.

Feel: whole front of the trunk.   Not: low back sagging, shoulders shrugging.

Watch the form

Pilates Chest Liftthe one rectus abdominis exercise of the day

Curl the ribs. Don’t yank the neck.

Sets 2Reps 8–12
  • Feet down.
  • Pelvis quiet.
  • Small curl — this is not a sit-up.
  • Lower with control.

Feel: front of the belly shortening.   Not: neck pulling, hip flexors gripping.

Watch the form

Side Planklateral support

Stack ribs over hips.

Sets 1–2Hold 15–25 sec / side
  • Knees down is fine.
  • No sagging into the shoulder.

Shoulder note
If the bottom shoulder complains, drop to knees or shorten the hold.

Watch the form
BUILD

Legs

16–18 min

A1Band Front Squatmain lower-body work

Stay tall. Push the floor away.

Sets 3Reps 10–18Band heavyPace 3 down · 1 hold · stand strong

Anchor / setupBand under both feet. Hold the ends up at your shoulders, elbows forward.

  • Stand on the band.
  • Hold it at the shoulders.
  • Sit down into the squat.
  • Keep ribs stacked.

Feel: thighs and glutes.   Not: the band folding you forward.

Watch the form

A2Band Squat Pulse or Split Squatextra quad and glute volume

Small range. No bouncing.

Sets 2Reps 12–20Band medium or heavy

Anchor / setupPulses: band under both feet, held at shoulders. Split squat: band under the front foot only, held at shoulders.

  • Stay in the clean part of the squat.
  • Keep tension on the band the whole time.
  • Stop before the knees or back get cranky.

Knee note
Shorten the range if the knee feels sharp.

Watch the form

A3Band RDL / Good Morninghamstrings and glutes 🌱 fertilizer

Hips back. Spine long.

Sets 3Reps 12–18Band heavyPace 3 down · 2 stretch · controlled up

Anchor / setupBand under both feet. Hold the ends at your hips (RDL) or up at your shoulders (good morning) — never across the neck.

  • Stand on the band.
  • Hold the ends at hips or shoulders.
  • Hinge, don’t squat.
  • Stop before the back rounds.

Feel: hamstring stretch.   Not: low-back strain.

Back note
Don’t chase the floor. If your back rounds, you went too low.

Watch the form

A4Band Frog Pump or Glute Bridgeglute burn 🌱 fertilizer

Squeeze first. Range second.

Sets 2Reps 20–30Band medium or heavy

Anchor / setupLoop above the knees, or a long band across the hips with the ends anchored under both feet.

  • Use a loop above the knees or a long band across the hips.
  • Keep the move small if needed.
  • The low back should not be the star.

Feel: glutes.   Not: low back cramping.

Watch the form
BUILD

Push

8–10 min

B1Band Chest Pressmain push

Press from a stacked rib cage.

Sets 4Reps 10–20Band mediumPace 3 return · press strong

Anchor / setupBand around your upper back, one end in each hand. Your own back is the anchor — nothing to attach.

  • Band behind the upper back.
  • Press forward.
  • Return slowly.
  • Keep the shoulders heavy.

Feel: chest and front shoulder.   Not: ribs flaring to make the press easier.

Shoulder note
Keep elbows slightly below shoulder height if the shoulders complain.

Watch the form

B2Band Push-Up or Press-Outextra push volume

Clean reps beat dramatic reps.

Sets 2Reps 8–15Band light or medium

Anchor / setupPush-up: band across the upper back, ends pinned under both palms. Press-out: no anchor — hold one end in each hand at your chest and press straight out.

  • If doing band push-ups, the band goes across the upper back.
  • If that’s too much, do standing band press-outs instead.
  • Screw your hands into the floor. Push the floor away.
  • Stop before the shoulders or wrists complain.

Wrist note
Fists or push-up handles if the wrists object. Press-outs skip the wrist load entirely.

Watch the form
BUILD

Pull

8–10 min

C1Band Rowmain pull

Squeeze, then return like you’re in charge.

Sets 4Reps 12–20Band medium or heavyPace 2 pull · 2 squeeze · 3 return

Anchor / setupSeated with the band around both feet, or standing hinged with it under both feet. A safe hotel-room anchor (door anchor, sturdy post) works if you have one.

  • Anchor with your feet, or a safe hotel-room anchor.
  • Pull the elbows back.
  • Squeeze the shoulder blades — juice out of the mango.
  • Return slowly.

Feel: mid-back.   Not: biceps doing all the work.

Anchor note
Test any door or post anchor with a few gentle pulls before you load it.

Watch the form

Pull your shoulder blades back into the conversation.

Sets 1–2Reps 15–25Band light

Anchor / setupNo anchor. Hold the band in both hands at chest height, arms straight.

  • Chest height.
  • Pull apart.
  • Pause.
  • Slow return — don’t let the band snap you back.

Feel: upper back.   Not: neck. Heavy shoulders — your neck is still not invited.

Your Choice

3–5 min

Familiar work today. You get to pick how it ends.

If you feel good — end standingpride, completion, “I did that”

  • Horse stance — 20–30 sec. Push the floor apart. watch
  • Tall standing breath — 3 breaths. Grow taller on each one.

If you feel cooked — end relaxedsettle the body

  • Forward fold — 30 sec. Don’t chase the floor. Chase the position. watch
  • 90/90 stretch — 30 sec / side. watch
  • Hero stretch — 30 sec / side. Breathe where you’re tight. watch

When you repeat this on Day 7

Do not add exercises. Pick one progression per exercise:

  • Use the next heavier band.
  • Shorten the band (step wider, or choke up on the ends).
  • Add a 2-second squeeze at the hard end.
  • Add a 3-second return.
  • Keep tension between reps — never go slack.
  • Add 2–5 reps.
  • Reduce transition time, only if form stays clean.

Adjust range and depth based on how your knee, hip, shoulder, and back feel. Bands store energy — never let one snap back at you, and check any door anchor before you load it. Check with a professional first if anything hurts or you're unsure this suits you.
