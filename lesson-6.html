Lesson 06 · Travel Workout 2

Pack Light,
Train Hard.

This one proves the point: bands can replace the workout. Not a substitute, not a holding pattern — a complete session in a hotel room, out of a backpack, with nothing bolted to the floor.

Lesson 3 taught you to make a band feel heavy. Lesson 6 asks you to run a whole balanced day on that skill alone.

PrincipleBands can replace the workout.
RoleBalanced full body.
Time40–55 minutes.
EquipmentLight, medium, and heavy long bands or tubes. A safe anchor if you have one.
MoodTravel · familiar · self-directed.
Need the band rules? Tap here

Which band for what:

  • Light band — shoulders, pull-aparts, lateral raises.
  • Medium band — chest press, overhead press, face pulls.
  • Heavy band — squats, rows, good mornings, glute bridges.
  • A set only counts if the last few reps are hard and clean.
  • 25 easy reps = the band is too light or too slack. Step wider, shorten the band, take out slack.

Equipment note. This workout assumes long bands or tubes. Packed only mini-loops? The squats, rows, presses, and good mornings need a swap — the anchor lines won't work as written.

How travel week works

Two workouts, each done twice. Not four new workouts.

Day 1Create Tension
Day 2Rest — walk, swim, shop, enjoy the trip
Day 3Pack Light, Train Hard
Day 4Rest — fun movement
Day 5Create Tension — progressed
Day 6Rest — fun movement
Day 7Pack Light, Train Hard — progressed

"Progressed" never means new exercises. Pick one per exercise: next heavier band · shorten the band · add a 2-second squeeze · add a 3-second return · keep tension between reps · add 2–5 reps · shorten transitions only if form stays clean.

Both workouts once ≈ 10 hard sets per major muscle group. Both twice ≈ 20. That's enough. Don't turn your trip into punishment.

Step 1 / 7GET LOW

Move Prep

5 min

This is where your wine lives — low, loose, and in control, without your knees or back paying for it later. Same warm-up every lesson: learn it once, own it forever.

Before you begin

Today's warm-up prepares your body for today's workout — not for a flexibility contest.

  • Work only in a pain-free range.
  • Hold a chair, wall, or countertop anytime balance limits the movement.
  • Smaller movements done well beat bigger movements done poorly.
  • If something feels sharp, unstable, or wrong, reduce the range or skip that exercise and move on.

Squat & Pistol Prepeasy and controlled — warm, not tired

  • Wide deep-squat hold — 20–30 seconds. watch Mod: hold a chair or countertop, or don't squat as low.
  • Weight shifts in the wide squat — rock side to side, 8 each side. watch
  • Cossack shift (a.k.a. spider-man lunge) — stay low, let your hips travel side to side, 6–8 each way. Keep the working heel down; don't force depth. watchMod: stand a little taller, or hold a chair for balance.
  • Narrow deep-squat hold — 20 seconds. watch Mod: hold support, or place a rolled towel under your heels.
  • Staggered deep-squat hold — 15–20 seconds each side. watch Mod: shorten the stance and reduce the depth.
  • Knee-over-toe touches — 8–10 each side, 2-second pause. watchMod: let the knee travel only as far as comfortable, or hold a chair.
  • Assisted one-leg squat practice (hold something) — 3–5 clean each side. watchMod: use both hands for support, or squat to a higher chair.
  • Shoulder-blade push-ups — 10 reps. watchMod: perform against a wall, countertop, or bench.
  • Band pull-aparts or big shoulder circles — 15 reps. watchMod: use a lighter band, or reduce the range of motion.

Ready to build? If you're breathing a little harder and your joints feel smoother — not tired — you warmed up just right.

Then, band-day add-on

Grab your bands and do a few light warm-up reps of today's first move — a couple of easy banded squats or rows — just to feel the tension before you load it. New tool, same body.

Push

8–10 min

B1Band Chest Pressmain push

Press from a stacked rib cage.

Sets 4Reps 10–20Band mediumPace 3 return · press strong

Anchor / setupBand around your upper back, one end in each hand. Your own back is the anchor — nothing to attach.

  • Band behind the upper back.
  • Press forward.
  • Return slowly.
  • Keep the shoulders heavy.

Feel: chest and front shoulder.   Not: ribs flaring to make the press easier.

Shoulder note
Keep elbows slightly below shoulder height if the shoulders complain.

Watch the form

B2Band Push-Up or Press-Outextra push volume

Clean reps beat dramatic reps.

Sets 2Reps 8–15Band light or medium

Anchor / setupPush-up: band across the upper back, ends pinned under both palms. Press-out: no anchor — hold one end in each hand at your chest and press straight out.

  • If doing band push-ups, the band goes across the upper back.
  • If that’s too much, do standing band press-outs instead.
  • Screw your hands into the floor. Push the floor away.
  • Stop before the shoulders or wrists complain.

Wrist note
Fists or push-up handles if the wrists object. Press-outs skip the wrist load entirely.

Watch the form

When you repeat this on Day 7

Do not add exercises. Pick one progression per exercise:

  • Use the next heavier band.
  • Shorten the band (step wider, or choke up on the ends).
  • Add a 2-second squeeze at the hard end.
  • Add a 3-second return.
  • Keep tension between reps — never go slack.
  • Add 2–5 reps.
  • Reduce transition time, only if form stays clean.
